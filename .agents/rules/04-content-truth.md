# Rule 04 — Copy, voice and truth (always on)

## Truth
- Every number, client, price, address, year and claim comes from `content/site-facts.ts` (mirror of `docs/site-facts.md`). If you need a fact that isn't there, write `[[CONFIRM: what you need]]` and add it to `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`. Never invent.
- Testimonials render only items with `verified: true` and `permission: true`. Never write a testimonial, review, rating, client quote or client name yourself — not even as a placeholder that ships. If there are fewer than 3 verified testimonials, render the Proof Wall instead.
- Never show: internal dashboards with real financial data, ROAS/ROI tiles, client names/logos without permission, bank details, street addresses outside Pakistan.
- The team is in-house in Pakistan; present every listed service as delivered by the Webix team. Do not claim offices in Canada, the US, the UAE or New Zealand — say "clients in" / "markets we serve".
- Prices come from `content/pricing.ts`. Always say ad spend is paid by the client directly to the platform.
- No guarantees of rankings, "#1 on Google", "guaranteed leads", or "get recommended by ChatGPT". We make businesses reachable, readable and accurately described.

## Voice
- Plain, confident, specific English. Short sentences. Founder-level: talk about outcomes, timelines, ownership and prices.
- Answer first, then detail. Prefer numbers to adjectives ("live in 14 days" beats "fast turnaround").
- Address the reader as "you"; the company as "we".
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. Buttons say exactly what happens: "Book a strategy call", "See packages", "Send message", "Book this time".

## Banned phrases
unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, supercharge, skyrocket, game-changer, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, next level, in today's digital landscape / fast-paced world, one-stop shop, tailored solutions (without saying what), seamless, synergy, leverage (as a verb), we're passionate, results-driven, best-in-class, world-class, revolutionize, journey (for a sales process), "Look no further", "Whether you're X or Y".

## Page copy structure
- H1 says what the page offers in plain words with the primary keyword.
- The 2 sentences under H1: what it is, who it's for, typical price or timeline.
- Sections answer real buyer questions: what do I get, how long, how much, who owns it, what do you need from me, what happens next.
- FAQs: 6–8 real questions; first sentence of each answer answers it directly.
- Every page ends with one clear next step (booking).

## Forms & microcopy
- Labels above inputs, never placeholder-only. Errors say what's wrong and how to fix it ("Enter an email like name@company.com").
- Success states confirm the exact action ("You're booked for Tue 4 Nov, 11:00 AM Toronto time").
- Empty/failed states give an alternative (WhatsApp, email).
